The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first (en-GB, en-IE, de-DE, fr-FR, nl-NL and the rest on file) gets day and month swapped: 4 March becomes 3 April. It comes in through the app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms. This is the bug #4471 was named for.

hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. The tenant’s people type month first because the hint tells them to, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as parser (a swap), so subject-line dedupe merged it. Fixing the parser does not fix it; the hint and the form have to agree. Only day-first tenants on booking-v3, only through the form.

one-day: the invite lands one day off. No month and day swap at all: the date moves by one day, or differs between people in different places (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). That points at time zone handling when the invite is made, not at reading the date. The desk already noted these (desk-notes.json); the table files them under parser and footnotes them.

Why it happened

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. Every problem here produces the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”), so subject dedupe cannot tell them apart. The bodies and the tenant can.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The “tagged as” column shows how often it disagrees with the cause. Don’t triage on it.
  • The dedupe pages were last reviewed in 2024 and show as stale.
